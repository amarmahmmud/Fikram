TechBuddy Customer Support Guide
==================================

TechBuddy is a friendly tech support company that helps small businesses with their IT needs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
---------------------------

Q: What are your support hours?
A: TechBuddy is available Monday through Friday, 8am to 6pm. For emergencies, we have 24/7 on-call support for premium subscribers.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and check your inbox for a reset link. The link expires in 30 minutes.

Q: My computer is running slow. What should I do?
A: First, restart your computer. Then check for Windows updates, clear your browser cache, and make sure you have at least 10% free disk space. If the problem continues, contact our support team.

Q: How do I set up a new email account?
A: We support Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail. To set up a new account, go to Settings, select Add Account, choose your email provider, and enter your credentials. We recommend enabling two-factor authentication.

Q: What is your pricing?
A: Our Basic plan is $29/month for up to 5 users. The Professional plan is $79/month for up to 25 users. Enterprise plans are custom-quoted. All plans include unlimited support tickets.

Q: How do I back up my files?
A: We recommend the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies of your data, on 2 different media types, with 1 copy off-site. We offer automated cloud backup starting at $9/month.

Q: My internet is not working. What should I do?
A: Start by restarting your router — unplug it, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. If that doesn't work, check if others in the office are affected. If so, contact your ISP. If only your device is affected, try forgetting and rejoining the Wi-Fi network.

Q: Can you help with cybersecurity?
A: Yes! We offer security audits, antivirus setup, firewall configuration, staff phishing training, and monthly security reports. Cybersecurity packages start at $49/month.

Q: How do I share files with my team?
A: We recommend using Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Dropbox. We can help set up shared folders, manage permissions, and train your team. File sharing setup is included in all our plans.

Q: What should I do if I get a virus?
A: Disconnect from the internet immediately. Do not enter any passwords or sensitive information. Contact our emergency line and we will remotely scan and clean your system within 2 hours.

Q: Do you offer remote support?
A: Yes! Most issues can be resolved remotely. Our technicians use secure screen-sharing tools to diagnose and fix problems on your computer. Remote sessions are free for all plan subscribers.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: You can cancel anytime from your account dashboard under Billing, or email billing@techbuddy.com. Your service continues until the end of the billing period with no cancellation fees.

Q: What is two-factor authentication and do I need it?
A: Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second security step when logging in, usually a code sent to your phone. We strongly recommend enabling it on all business accounts. We can help set it up for free.

Q: My printer is not working. Can you help?
A: Yes. Common fixes include restarting the printer and computer, reinstalling the printer driver, or clearing the print queue. Our technicians can diagnose printer issues remotely or on-site.

Q: How long does it take to resolve a support ticket?
A: Most tickets are resolved within 4 business hours. Critical issues affecting your entire business are escalated and addressed within 1 hour. You will receive email updates as your ticket progresses.

Q: Do you offer on-site support?
A: Yes, on-site visits are available for complex issues that cannot be resolved remotely. On-site visits are $99 per hour with a 1-hour minimum. Scheduling is usually available within 24 hours.

Q: Can you help us move to the cloud?
A: Absolutely. We specialize in cloud migrations for small businesses. We help plan, execute, and train your team on tools like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and AWS. Cloud migration projects start at $499.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfer. All payments are processed securely and receipts are emailed automatically.
